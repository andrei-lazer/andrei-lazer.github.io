Physics-informed neural nets

This is a project completed as part of my master's. I investigated physics-informed neural nets and applied various methods to help in learning highly oscillatory and stiff equations. I enjoyed this project more than most others because my group was very supportive, and the work was interdisciplinary. I'm particularly proud of the personal extension, which applied augmented Lagrangian methods to minimising the loss function.
