## Types of networks by geography
- **LANs**: Small area like an office or school. Can be through ethernet, or through WiFi (WLAN).
- **MANs**: Metropolitan Area Network, spans the size of a city or a large university campus.
- **WANs**: Wireless Area Networks. Interconnection across large distances, like cities or countries. Usually use fibre-optic.
